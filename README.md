# Data-Science-Internship-Report
A Comparative Study on ESG Practices in Selected Indian Corporates.

This study aims to develop a sustainability framework to evaluate the overall sustainability performance of organizations. It focuses particularly on a set of organizations, assessing their performance across three core dimensions: Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG). The report presents a comprehensive review of the sustainability status of these enterprises and examines relevant literature to build a conceptual understanding of corporate sustainability and related constructs. The study is based on secondary data analysis and concludes by summarizing the findings in light of the research objectives.
